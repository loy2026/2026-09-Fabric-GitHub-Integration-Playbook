Part VI · Operations and adoption

21Implementation roadmap

Sequencing matters here more than usual, because two of these phases are safety nets for the ones that follow. Phase 0 exists so that Phase 1 cannot lose anything; Phase 2 exists so that Phase 3's isolation is real rather than nominal.

Rule

Do not reorder phases 0 to 2

Phase 0 is the safety net for Phase 1's destructive potential. Phase 2 is what makes Phase 3's isolation real. A team that goes straight to branch-out has developers working in workspaces that write to shared data, which is worse than not having branch workspaces at all — because everyone believes they are isolated.

Localize

Sequence, do not schedule, in this document

Durations depend on estate size, item count, how much hard-coding exists and how quickly administrative requests are served. Record your own dates in Appendix G. The dependency order above is the part that transfers between organizations.
